The story

Money help in the UAE should be on your side

goodground started with a simple question that should have an easy answer: what can I actually afford? Here is why we built it, and who is behind it.

Why goodground exists

Most money tools in the UAE are built to sell you something. A calculator that ends in a loan offer. A comparison site paid by the cards it ranks. An app that needs your salary, your phone number and your Emirates ID before it tells you anything useful.

Meanwhile the questions people actually have go unanswered. How much should I really save? What will buying a home cost on top of the price? Which debt should I pay first? Where do I even start with investing?

goodground is the tool we wished existed: honest answers about your own money, in plain language, with nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy.

Who is behind it

Built by someone who designed the other side

goodground was started by Malak Usama, a product designer who has spent years designing financial products, including mortgage and lending journeys at Revolut.

Working on the inside showed her how much of the hard part, the jargon, the hidden costs, the fine print, lands on the person least equipped to deal with it. goodground takes the same care that goes into the best banking products and points it at you instead.

What we believe

Clarity is kindness

A clear number and a next step beat a page of caveats. We show our working, then get out of the way.

Built for life here

Rent cheques, gratuity, visa rules, Central Bank limits. UAE money works differently, so our tools do too.

Your numbers, your call

We never push a product or a lender. You get the picture, and the decision stays with you.

Start with what matters to you

Pick a goal and get a clear plan in about two minutes. Nothing is saved.
