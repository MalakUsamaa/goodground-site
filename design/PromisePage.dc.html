Our promise

We don’t sell you anything, and we never will.

Most money tools exist to pass your details to someone selling a loan, a card or an investment. goodground gives you honest answers about your own money, and nothing else. Here is exactly what that means.

Independent

We do not recommend lenders, banks or products, and nobody pays us to rank them.

Private

No sign up and no account. Your answers stay in your browser and disappear when you close the page.

Honest numbers

Every plan shows the assumptions behind it. Every lesson lists its sources and the date we checked them.

No sales calls

We will never pass your details to anyone or call you to sell you something.

Plain language

If we cannot explain it simply, we have not understood it well enough yet.

Free to use

Every planner and every course is free. No trial, no paywall, no catch.

What we will never do

Some lines we will not cross

If anything about how goodground works ever changes, we will say so on this page first.

  • Sell or share your data
  • Take commission for sending you to a lender
  • Ask for your Emirates ID, salary certificate or bank login
  • Hide a cost in the small print
  • Tell you what to buy
